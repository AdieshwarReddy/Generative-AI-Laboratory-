1.What is DCGAN?A Deep Convolutional Generative Adversarial Network that uses convolutional neural networks for image generation.



2.What is the difference between GAN and DCGAN?A basic GAN may use fully connected layers, while DCGAN uses convolutional and transposed convolutional layers to model spatial image structure.



3.What are the two components of DCGAN?Generator and Discriminator.



4.What is the role of the Generator?To convert random noise vectors into synthetic images.



5.What is the role of the Discriminator?To distinguish real images from generated images.



6.What is Conv2DTranspose?A transposed convolution layer commonly used to increase the spatial resolution of feature maps.



7.What is Conv2D?A convolutional layer that extracts spatial features such as edges and shapes from images.



8.Why is Batch Normalization used?It normalizes intermediate activations and can improve training stability.



9.Why is LeakyReLU used?It allows a small gradient for negative inputs, reducing the risk of inactive neurons.



10.Why is Tanh used in the Generator output?It produces values between −1 and 1, matching the normalized MNIST pixel range.



11.Why does the Discriminator output a logit?The raw score is used with binary cross-entropy configured with from_logits=True.



12.What is adversarial training?A process where the Generator learns to fool the Discriminator and the Discriminator learns to distinguish real from fake images.



13.What is mode collapse?A GAN failure where the Generator produces a limited variety of outputs instead of diverse samples.



14.What is the MNIST dataset?A dataset of 70,000 grayscale handwritten digit images of size 28 × 28 pixels.



15.What are applications of DCGAN?Synthetic image generation, data augmentation, image synthesis, and generative AI research.
